Junior Data Analyst

Responsibilities:
- Analyze business data and prepare reports and dashboards.
- Use Python, SQL, pandas and Excel to clean and explore datasets.
- Build visualizations with Power BI or Tableau.
- Collaborate with teams and communicate insights clearly.
- Support machine learning experiments and model evaluation.

Requirements:
- Strong analytical thinking and problem solving.
- Knowledge of Python, SQL, data analysis, statistics and data visualization.
- Familiarity with Git and cloud platforms such as AWS is an advantage.
- Good communication and teamwork.
